$ \sin\left(\sin^{-1}(7)\right) = 7 $

$ \sin^{-1}\left(\sin(7)\right) = 7 $
(3.) Determine the first exact value of $\sin^{-1}\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$


$ \sin^{-1}\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) \\[5ex] = 240^\circ ... Unit\:\: Circle\:\: Trigonometry \\[3ex] = 240 * \dfrac{\pi}{180} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{4\pi}{3} $
